# Fictional transport and vehicle policy

Naija Chase does not depict a real airline, bus company, taxi platform, vehicle
manufacturer, or logistics company as transporting a thief or participating in
crime. Gameplay uses generic descriptions or fictional operators until formal
advertising, trademark, legal, and editorial policies exist.

Real institutions may appear only in separately reviewed factual educational
material where the reference is necessary and neutral. They must not appear as
interactive travel operators, purchasable vehicles, suspect vehicles, or
criminal associates. The automated content gate rejects the known transport
brands audited during P3.7; editorial review remains required for new content.
