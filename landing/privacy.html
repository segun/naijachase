1. Who we are

Naija Chase (“we”, “us”, “our”) is a free online detective game set in Nigeria. We are the data controller for personal data processed through the Naija Chase website at naijachase.com (the “Website”) and the game at naijachase.app (the “Game”). Together these are the “Service”.

You can contact us about privacy at support@naijachase.com.

2. What this policy covers

This policy explains what personal data we process when you visit the Website, create an account, or play the Game, why we process it, who receives it, and what rights you have. We process personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (the “NDPA”) and, where it applies to you, other data protection laws.

3. Data we collect

3.1 The Website (naijachase.com)

The Website is a static information site. It does not require an account, does not set cookies and does not contain analytics or advertising scripts. Like any web server, ours automatically records basic technical data when a page is requested: your IP address, the time of the request, the page requested, your browser’s user-agent string and the response status. See section 3.4.

3.3 Data created while you play

  • Your cases, the stops you visit, the witnesses you question, your travel choices, your dossier entries, warrants, arrests and results.
  • Your case budget use, scores, career points, rank, achievements, tokens earned in the Game and weekly-challenge results.
  • Timestamps of actions, so the Game can apply deadlines, travel times and anti-cheating checks.

This is gameplay data about a fictional investigation. We do not use it to profile you for advertising.

3.4 Technical and security data

  • Your IP address, browser and device type, and the dates and times of requests, recorded in server logs.
  • Session information for each device you sign in on (for example when a session was created and last used, and the browser or device type) so you can review and sign out of your devices.
  • Request counts used for rate limiting and abuse prevention.

3.5 Data we do not collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, payment card details, government identification numbers or biometric data. We do not currently run advertising, and checkout is not enabled.

Under the NDPA we must have a lawful basis for processing. Ours are:

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

6. Who we share it with

We share personal data only where needed to run the Service:

  • Email delivery. We use Cloudflare’s email sending service to deliver account emails. Cloudflare receives your email address and the content of the message, which is a confirmation or password-reset link.
  • Hosting and infrastructure. Our servers, database, backups and network providers process data on our behalf under confidentiality and security obligations.
  • Other players. If you join a private group, its members can see your username, avatar and weekly results in that group. Groups are private and invitation-only. We do not publish your email address to other players.
  • Authorities and legal reasons. We may disclose data when required by law, court order or a lawful request, or to protect the rights, safety and property of our users, the public or ourselves.
  • Business changes. If the Service is transferred to another operator, your data may be transferred too. We will tell you first and the same protections will continue to apply.

Our service providers may use your data only for our instructions and not for their own purposes, except where they act as independent controllers as the law requires.

7. International transfers

Some of our providers, including our email provider, may process data outside Nigeria. Where we transfer personal data abroad, we do so only where the NDPA allows it, for example to a country or provider that gives an adequate level of protection, or under appropriate safeguards such as contractual protections.

8. How long we keep it

  • Account and gameplay data: for as long as your account exists. Inactive accounts that have not been used for 24 months may be deleted after we notify you by email.
  • Server logs and security data: up to 90 days, unless we need them longer to investigate abuse or a security incident.
  • Sessions: until they expire or you sign out. Revoked sessions are removed in the ordinary course.
  • Backups: deleted data may remain in routine encrypted backups for up to 90 days until they are overwritten. We will not restore deleted data except to recover from a failure, and will re-delete it if we do.
  • Legal claims and obligations: we may keep specific records for longer where the law requires or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

When you delete your account we remove or irreversibly anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except what we must keep as described above.

9. Security

We protect personal data with measures that include hashing passwords with Argon2id, opaque revocable session tokens, rotating sessions and revoking a whole session family if an old token is reused, HTTPS for all connections, rate limiting, restricted access to servers and databases, and keeping secrets out of source code. The Game also keeps answers and hidden case information on the server and not in your browser. No system is completely secure, and you should choose a strong, unique password and sign out on shared devices. If a personal data breach is likely to put your rights at risk we will notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission and, where required, you, within the time the law requires.

10. Your rights

Under the NDPA you may have the right to:

  • be informed about how we use your data (this policy);
  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • ask us to delete your data, or restrict its use;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format (portability);
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier lawful processing; and
  • not be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing that significantly affects you.

To use any of these rights, email support@naijachase.com from the address registered to your account. We may ask you to verify your identity first. We will respond within one month, or sooner if the law requires. We will not charge a fee unless a request is clearly unfounded or excessive. You can also change your avatar and sign out of any device from inside the Game.

11. Children

The Service is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you are under 18, you may use the Service only with the permission and supervision of a parent or guardian, who consents to our handling of your data on your behalf. If we learn that we hold data of a child under 13, or of a minor without the required parental consent, we will delete it. Parents and guardians who believe this has happened can contact us at support@naijachase.com.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices or the law change, for example if we add paid cosmetic items, new features or analytics. We will update the date at the top. If a change is material, we will notify you by email or inside the Game before it takes effect, and ask for consent where the law requires it.

13. Contact and complaints

Questions or requests: support@naijachase.com.

If you believe we have not handled your data properly and we have not resolved your concern, you have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng). If you live elsewhere, you may also contact your local data protection authority.
