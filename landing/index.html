What is Naija Chase?

You are the detective. Nigeria is the crime scene.

Every case starts with a theft and a thief who is already on the move. You will not be told where they went. You find out by listening, thinking and knowing your country.

🗣️

Question the witnesses

At every stop you meet three people: a trader, a driver, a guard, a boatman. One of them saw the thief and hands you a fresh clue. The others know the place, so listen carefully to who is worth trusting.

🧭

Work out where they went

Witnesses never name the destination. They talk about food, festivals, landmarks, industry, weather and history. Put three clues together and only one state fits. Then you travel there, by road or by air, and the clock keeps running.

📋

Build the dossier

Along the way witnesses describe the thief: sex, height, skin tone, hair, hobby, vehicle, accessory and one distinguishing feature. Record all eight details in your dossier to unlock the warrant.

🚨

Get the warrant. Make the arrest.

Pick the right suspect from three, then find the hideout. Arrive without a warrant and the thief slips away. You have until Monday 07:00 to bring them in.

What makes it different

Built around Nigeria, not just set in it.

The places, foods, festivals, industries and people in Naija Chase come from Nigeria itself. You will probably learn something about your own country, and about the ones next door.

🗺️

All 36 states and the FCT

Hundreds of locations, from Lagos waterfronts to Sokoto emirate courts, Osun sacred groves, Benue farms and Rivers refineries. Every place has its own atmosphere and opening hours.

🎬

Animated scenes

Witness encounters and chases play out as animated scenes. They use 3D where your device can handle it and switch to a lighter 2D view where it cannot, so the game never leaves you with a blank screen.

⏱️

Real time pressure

Travel and interviews cost time. Roads are quicker for short trips and flights for long ones, but your case budget is limited. Waste a day on a false lead and you may not recover it.

👥

Weekly challenges with friends

Create a private group, then race your friends on the same case each week. Groups are invitation-only. There is no public chat and nothing to discover from strangers.

Questions

Before you start

What is Naija Chase?

A browser-based detective game. You follow a thief across Nigeria by questioning witnesses, working out the next destination from their clues, collecting a description of the thief and finally making an arrest.

Is it free?

Yes. Naija Chase is free to play. Any optional extras that may be added in future will be cosmetic and will never affect how a case plays out.

Do I need to know Nigeria well?

It helps, but you do not need to be an expert. Clues start broad and get sharper, and many cases teach you something new about a state as you go.

Do I need an account?

Yes. You register with an email address, a username and a password, and confirm your email. That lets us save your progress and rank, and keep your cases safe across devices.

What do I need to play?

Any modern browser on a phone, tablet or computer, and an internet connection. There is nothing to download.

Are the crimes and people real?

No. All thieves, victims, businesses and incidents are invented. Real states, cities, landmarks and cultural facts are used so the game feels true to Nigeria. See our Disclaimer.

Is it suitable for children?

Naija Chase is a puzzle game without graphic violence. You must be at least 13 to create an account, and under-18s need a parent or guardian’s permission. Please see our Terms and Privacy Policy.

How do I contact you?

Email support@naijachase.com for help, feedback, account deletion or any privacy request.
